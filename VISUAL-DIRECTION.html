NicFree — visual direction
Visual direction

NicFree

A trail marker you add one stone to and never take one from. The interface has one job: to be trustworthy on a bad day.

Principles

One idea per screen. The home screen answers a single question and nothing else competes with the answer.

Nothing celebrates. No confetti, no badges, no fanfare. An app that cheers loudly has nothing decent to say when things go wrong, and that is the day that matters.

Typography instead of decoration. The hero number is the only large element in the app. Everything else recedes.

A slip is never red. Red is alarm, and alarm is shame. Slips are recorded in weathered clay, the colour of something noted rather than something failed.

Palette

Wet slate, lichen, weathered clay

Drawn from the object the app is named after rather than from health app convention. The neutrals carry a cold blue bias so that the single green accent reads as chosen rather than decorative.

Type

Three roles, no more

Set in the platform's own faces. The system serif carries anything that needs to sound like a person talking, which is exactly the copy that matters most: their own reasons, and the line after a slip.

Motion and feel

Slow, and almost absent

One animation exists in the whole app: the breathing circle on the craving screen, eight seconds per cycle, which is a paced breathing exercise rather than an ornament. It stops entirely under Reduce Motion and becomes a count.

Numbers change with a numeric transition rather than a bounce. Haptics are a single soft tap, and the success haptic is never used for logging a slip, because a system sound that means "well done" firing at that moment would be grotesque.

Deliberately absent

What we are not building

No

Streaks as the hero. The number that matters cannot reset.

No

Daily check in. A free day is the absence of a record, so silence counts in your favour.

No

Community or chat. QuitNow's own users call theirs unmoderated and hostile, and one developer cannot police it.

No

Badges, levels, mascots or confetti. That territory belongs to Kwit and it is the wrong promise.

No

Subscription, countdown timers or fake discounts. One price, stated once.

No

Accounts, analytics or third party code. Nothing leaves the device except your own iCloud.

No

Health claims without a source. QuitNow ships one that is wrong by fifteen years.
