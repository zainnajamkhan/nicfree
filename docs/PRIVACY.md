# NicFree privacy policy (draft)

_Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLISHING]_

NicFree does not collect any personal data.

**What stays on your iPhone.** Everything you enter in NicFree (your quit date, what you
used, what you spend, your reasons, cravings and slips) is stored only on your iPhone. It
is not sent to us or to anyone else.

**No account, no tracking.** NicFree has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no
third-party code. It does not track you across apps or websites.

**Purchases.** If you buy the unlock, Apple handles the payment. We never see your
payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the unlock has been bought.

**Notifications.** Milestone notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent
from a server.

**Deleting your data.** Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a
new date.
