How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Insights tab, tap See what unlocks, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Why didn't my free days reset after a slip?

On purpose. NicFree counts your free days in total, not a streak. A slip adds a used day; it never takes away the free days you already have.

Do I need to open the app every day?

No. Every day counts as free unless you log a slip. If you're away for a few days, NicFree asks about them once when you're back. There's an optional evening check-in in Settings, off unless you turn it on.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes your history, so think twice before you do.

Is NicFree medical advice?

No. NicFree is not a medical device. If you're worried about nicotine withdrawal, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.
