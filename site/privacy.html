Privacy policy

NicFree · Last updated October 22, 2026

NicFree does not collect any personal data.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter in NicFree (your quit date, what you used, what you spend, your reasons, cravings and slips) is stored only on your iPhone or iPad. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

No account, no tracking

NicFree has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

If you buy the unlock, Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the unlock has been bought.

Notifications

Milestone notifications are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
